Body: ox leather. Lining: canvas. Hardware: brass-finish knob closure.

Leather is a natural material. Grain and tone vary from piece to piece, and the surface develops a patina with use — this is the point, not a flaw.

Keep the bag dry. If it gets wet, pat with a soft cloth and air-dry away from direct heat. Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth. Store stuffed, in its dust bag, away from direct sunlight.

Treat it as leather, not as the object it resembles.
