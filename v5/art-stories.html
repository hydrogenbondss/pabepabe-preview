Art Stories

Collections are numbered like exhibitions, because that is how we think of them. A reverse walk through the rooms so far — from the current show back to the first boot.

01 Collection 10: Living/Room · Now showing

Living/Room

Household objects, rehomed as bags. A door knob promoted to closure. A socket that powers nothing and holds everything. Living/Room is the current exhibition — the KNOB series in leather, suede and canvas, sized for the daily inventory.

Every piece opens, closes and carries. The room it lives in is up to you.

View the collection
02 Collection 07: Sound Practice · Paris Fashion Week

Sound Practice

A collection of instruments that do not play, presented as a concert that did. Sound Practice was shown at Paris Fashion Week with Fashion Farm Foundation — ocarinas, lyres, kalimbas and a wooden fish, remade in leather and performed live.

The instruments kept their shapes and lost their voices. The bags kept everything else.

Meet the Sound Objects
03 Gallery+Store: 62B Staunton Street · Ongoing programme

The Gallery+Store

Part exhibition space, part shop. The room at G/F 62B Staunton Street, Central, is lent to artists for shows while the bags live among the work, priced and on the wall. Past residents have filled it with painting, sculpture and sound; the objects stayed put through all of it.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 14:00–19:00. Walk-ins welcome. The door knob on the door is, as far as we know, just a door knob.

About the Gallery+Store
04 SS21: Rafter · From the archive

Rafter

The rainboot origins. Rafter looked at the working wardrobe of the factory floor — water boots, paint buckets, labour gloves — and rebuilt it in leather.

One boot, no foot. The Single Rainboot has been in the collection ever since, weather irrelevant.
